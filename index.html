03 / WHY OFFSCRIPT EXISTS

Tech should make
room for everyone.

AI is shaping school, work, creativity, and everyday life. Everyone deserves the tools to understand it — not just the people building it.

Curiosity first.

Ask questions without feeling like you should already know the answers. That's where learning starts.

People over hype.

Look at who benefits, who might be harmed, and whose perspective is missing.

Access is essential.

Clear language, thoughtful design, and accessibility belong in technology from day one.

Your judgment matters.

AI can help you think. It shouldn't do all the thinking for you.

A REMINDER TO TAKE WITH YOU
