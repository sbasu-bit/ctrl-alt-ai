04 / THE NO-JARGON ZONE

Words worth knowing.

A tiny, searchable glossary. Clear explanations, no computer-science degree required.

8 TERMS
Algorithm

A set of steps or rules a computer follows to complete a task or solve a problem.

Model

A system trained on examples to recognize patterns and produce outputs, such as text or predictions.

Training data

The examples used during training to help a model learn patterns. The data influences what it can and cannot do.

Bias

A systematic pattern that can make an outcome unfair or less reliable for some people or situations.

Hallucination

When an AI system generates information that sounds plausible but is inaccurate or made up.

Prompt

The instructions or context a person gives an AI system to guide its response.

Generative AI

AI designed to create new content, such as text, images, audio, or code, based on learned patterns.

Privacy

Having meaningful control over personal information, including how it is collected, used, and shared.

FIELD NOTE 01 / UNDERSTAND

AI learns patterns — not meaning the way people do.

Many AI systems are trained using examples. During training, a model adjusts internal parameters to become better at producing outputs that fit patterns in its data and training objective.

  • Training data shapes what patterns a model can learn.
  • A model's output depends on its design, data, and context.
  • Fluent language is not proof of understanding or accuracy.
Takeaway: Ask what a system was trained to do, what information it may be missing, and where its limits matter.
